<h1>Sirens</h1>

  <p>A simple offline music player inspired by Spotify.</p>

  <p>
    Import your music as a <code>.zip</code> file, organize your tracks
    into playlists, and share your playlists with others.
  </p>

  <h2>Features</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Offline music playback</li>
    <li>Import music from <code>.zip</code> files</li>
    <li>Create and manage playlists</li>
    <li>Share playlists</li>
    <li>No internet connection required</li>
  </ul>

  <p>
    A small personal project built around offline music playback and
    playlist sharing.
  </p>
